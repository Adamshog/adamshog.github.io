Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about products, pricing, installations, repairs and contacting AGOER.

FAQs

Helpful information before you book

Do you sell plumbing and sanitary equipment?

Yes. AGOER is presented as a sanitary store and solutions provider. Contact the sales team to confirm product availability, specifications and current prices.

Do you offer installation and repair services?

Yes. Services include domestic and industrial plumbing, installation, maintenance and repair, water pumps, irrigation and other water systems.

How much does a plumbing job cost?

Pricing depends on the site, scope of work, materials, access and urgency. Request a quote with project details for an estimate.

Do you provide emergency plumbing?

Call the technical team on 0787 427563 or 0780 336933. Availability and response time must be confirmed by phone.

Can you visit my site before quoting?

A site assessment may be needed for complex installations. Contact AGOER to discuss the location and any applicable assessment fee.

Do you install boreholes, pumps and rainwater harvesting systems?

These are among the listed service areas. Contact the technical team to confirm the exact scope, site requirements and project schedule.

Which areas do you serve?

Contact either branch to confirm service coverage for your location.

How can I pay?

Payment methods, deposit requirements and invoicing arrangements should be agreed and confirmed in writing before work begins.
