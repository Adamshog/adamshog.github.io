Terms & Conditions

Basic terms for enquiries, quotations, bookings, cancellations, payments and service delivery.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Enquiries and quotations

Website enquiries are not confirmed bookings. A quotation should describe the scope, materials, labour, applicable taxes, estimated timeline and validity period where relevant.

2. Booking confirmation

A booking is confirmed only when AGOER and the customer agree on the scope, schedule, price and any deposit or other conditions in writing.

3. Site access and customer responsibilities

The customer should provide accurate site information, safe access, relevant permissions and access to water or power where agreed as necessary for the work.

4. Changes to scope

Additional work or changes that affect cost or timing should be discussed and approved by the customer before the additional work proceeds.

5. Payments

Payment amounts, methods, deposits, milestones and due dates must be stated in the accepted quotation or invoice. Customers should request an official receipt for payments.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

Customers should notify AGOER as early as possible if they need to cancel or reschedule. Any cancellation charges or non-refundable costs must be disclosed and agreed before booking.

7. Materials and workmanship

Product warranties, workmanship guarantees and exclusions should be stated in the quotation or invoice. Customers should retain proof of purchase and service documents.

8. Safety and limitations

Work may be paused where conditions are unsafe, access is unavailable or additional technical assessment is required. Emergency response times are not guaranteed unless expressly agreed.

9. Liability and disputes

Any liability limitations and dispute-resolution arrangements must comply with applicable law. Customers should raise concerns promptly so the parties can seek a practical resolution.

10. Updates

These terms may be updated. The terms applicable to a specific job should be those provided and accepted at the time of booking.

This is a general starter template, not legal advice. AGOER should obtain local legal review and insert its registered business details, warranty terms, payment methods and cancellation policy before launch.
