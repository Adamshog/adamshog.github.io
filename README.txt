AGOER SANITARY STORE & SOLUTIONS — WEBSITE PACKAGE
=================================================

FILES
- index.html: Home page
- about.html: Company introduction
- services.html: Detailed service list
- projects.html: Project showcase
- gallery.html: Photo gallery
- quote.html: Request-a-quote form
- emergency.html: Emergency contacts
- contact.html: Contact information and map
- faq.html: Frequently asked questions
- privacy.html: Privacy policy template
- terms.html: Terms and conditions template
- assets/style.css: Responsive website design
- assets/main.js: Mobile menu, homepage slider and quote form
- images/: Locally stored visual assets

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted AGOER-Sanitary-Store-and-Solutions folder.
3. Double-click index.html. The website runs in a modern browser; no PHP or database is required.

HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload the entire folder contents to your hosting provider's public_html (or website root) folder. Keep the assets and images folders in the same location as the HTML pages.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMISATIONS BEFORE LAUNCH
1. The images supplied are locally stored SVG starter illustrations, not photographs. Replace them with real AGOER project/product photos while keeping the same filenames, or update the image paths in the HTML. This session could not download third-party stock photos, so no external stock photo has been falsely presented as a local photo.
2. Replace the placeholder email info@agoerplumbingsolutions.com with AGOER's confirmed email in contact.html, footer links and assets/main.js.
3. Replace # social links in the footer with AGOER's official Facebook, Instagram and other profile URLs.
4. Replace the YouTube and TikTok links in index.html with the official AGOER channel/profile links.
5. Confirm branch opening hours, emergency availability, service coverage and any call-out fees.
6. Add verified company background, years of experience, qualifications, licences, warranties and real completed-project details. These have not been invented.
7. The quote form uses mailto and opens the visitor's email app. It does not store form submissions. For automatic online submissions, connect a form backend or hosting form service.

ADD PHOTOS TO THE GALLERY
- Copy JPG, PNG or WebP images into the images folder.
- Open gallery.html in a text editor.
- Copy one <figure> block and change its src filename and caption.
- Save and refresh the browser.
