Privacy Policy

How AGOER may collect, use and protect information shared through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

When you contact AGOER or request a quotation, you may provide your name, telephone number, email address, project location and details about the service you need.

2. How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange site visits, provide services, communicate about bookings and maintain business records.

3. Sharing

AGOER should only share personal information where needed to deliver a requested service, comply with applicable law or protect legitimate business interests. Information should not be sold to third parties.

4. Website forms

The current quote form is a static mailto form. It prepares an email in the visitor's own email application and does not send information to or store it on this website's server. The visitor chooses whether to send the email.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This starter website does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Embedded Google Maps and links to social platforms are operated by third parties and may be subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data security and retention

AGOER should take reasonable steps to protect customer information and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law.

7. Your choices

You may contact AGOER to ask about personal information you have provided, or request correction where appropriate.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact AGOER using the sales numbers on the Contact Us page. Replace the placeholder business email before publication.

This is a starter policy template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed and adapted to AGOER's actual data practices and applicable Ugandan law before publishing.
